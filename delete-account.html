Delete your Two of Us account

Two of Us by Aurel Interactive. Last updated: 13 November 2026.

In the app

  1. Open Two of Us and go to Profile.
  2. Choose My account, then Delete account.
  3. Confirm.

Without the app

Email jason.crasto7@gmail.com from the address on your account and ask us to delete it.

What gets deleted

  • Your login, your profile and your personal notes are removed straight away.
  • If nobody is left in your shared space, the space and its photos are deleted too.

What stays

If your partner still has the space, the memories you shared stay with them.
