Two of Us

Couples app & games · for Android

A private space for the two of you. Chat, share photos straight to each other's home screens, plan dates, keep your memories and play games together, whether you live together or far apart.

What's in it

  • Just the two of you: a private chat with voice notes, photos and a daily question to answer together.
  • Home screen widgets that show each other's latest photo, the distance between you and the days until you next meet.
  • Plans and date nights: ideas near you, a shared calendar, lists, wishlists and things to watch.
  • Games for two: classics, word games, sports, quizzes and a pet you look after together.
  • Memories: a timeline of your milestones, letters and the photos you've shared.
