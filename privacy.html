Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026.

Two of Us is a private space for two people. This policy explains what we keep, why, and the choices you have. "We" means Aurel Interactive, the developer of Two of Us.

What you give us

  • Your account: your email address, or the Google account you sign in with.
  • Your profile: the name, character or photo you choose, and your time zone.
  • What you add to your space: photos, messages, voice notes, letters, notes, lists, plans, places, expenses, savings goals, wishlists, games, drawings, songs and watchlists.
  • Personal notes: these are stored against your account and are not shared with your partner.

When we check your age for 18+ games, we check the date you enter and keep only the result. We do not store your date of birth.

Things you choose to use

  • Date ideas near you: only when you ask, the app reads your phone's rough location, to about a kilometre or mile. We round it further before looking up places nearby, and don't keep it with your account. Your phone remembers that rough area so suggestions can lean towards it.
  • How far apart you are: if you share your spot on the distance screen, your rough location (to about a kilometre) and the name of that area are saved in your shared space so your partner can see them. Sharing again replaces it, and it goes when the space is deleted.
  • Links you share into the app: we fetch the link's public preview (title, caption and picture) to work out what it is. To sort it, the preview may be sent to our AI provider, Anthropic, which doesn't use it to train its models. If you save it, a copy of the picture is kept with your photos.
  • Gift ideas: when you ask for gift ideas in Shopping, your question goes to Anthropic with what helps it suggest well: your first names, the sizes and likes you've each added, both wishlists, how long you've been together and your next big day. Anthropic doesn't use it to train its models. The conversation itself is kept only on your phone, and gifts you save are visible only to you.
  • Shopping links: ideas open a search on Amazon (amazon.in) carrying our Amazon Associates tag, so Amazon can tell the visit came from us. What you do on Amazon is covered by Amazon's own privacy notice.
  • Suggestions while typing: when you type a song, film or place, the words go to Apple's iTunes Search or OpenStreetMap's Photon search to find matches. Other suggestions come from your own phone.

What's collected automatically

  • A device token, so we can send you notifications.
  • Basic technical information our providers need to run the service, such as your IP address.

Who can see your content

Everything in your shared space can be seen by the person you link with. Nobody else can see it through the app. We don't sell your data, and we don't show advertising based on it.

Photos are stored at private, unguessable web addresses. Anyone who is given such an address could open that photo, so don't share them outside the app.

Your content is not end-to-end encrypted. It is encrypted in transit and at rest by our providers, and we would only access it to keep the service running or where the law requires it.

Services we use

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Storage, Cloud Functions and Cloud Messaging) stores your data and runs the service, on servers in the United States.
  • YouTube and the Internet Archive supply videos when you use Watch together. Those services receive your IP address and their own terms apply.
  • Anthropic reads the preview of links you share into the app, to sort them, and suggests gift ideas when you ask in Shopping.
  • Amazon receives your visit when you open a shopping link, which carries our Associates tag.
  • Apple (iTunes Search) and komoot (Photon, using OpenStreetMap data) answer searches for songs, films and places while you type, and for places near you.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Unlinking: you each keep a copy of what you shared. Your partner keeps theirs.
  • Deleting your account: go to Profile, then My account, then Delete account. Your account, profile and personal notes are removed straight away. Memories in a space you shared stay with your partner. If nobody is left in a space, it's deleted along with its photos.
  • You can also ask us to delete your data at jason.crasto7@gmail.com.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including the EU, the UK and California, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your data, and to object to how it's used. You can download a copy of your shared data from Profile, then Privacy & app lock. For anything else, contact us at jason.crasto7@gmail.com.

Children

Two of Us is not for children under 13 (or the minimum age where you live). 18+ games are limited to people who confirm they're adults and opt in.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and tell you in the app when the change matters.
